# Issue Triage Standard

## Goals
Classify and prioritize issues in a consistent way.

## Priority Levels

- P0: Critical, service or game-breaking.
- P1: High, major feature or noticeable bug.
- P2: Medium, regular defects or UX issues.
- P3: Low, cosmetic or minor enhancements.

Support and triage agents MUST assign a priority and domain for each issue.
