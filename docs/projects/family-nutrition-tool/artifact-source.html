About & Access

Scope of this project

This is the Berger family's shared kitchen tool: Jeremy, Natalie, and Marshall (4). It tracks what's in the pantry, the family's recipes, a weekly meal plan, and each person's dietary needs and preferences — so anyone planning a meal (a person or a connected Claude session) starts from the same real information instead of guessing.

Who's eating what

Jeremy is on a low-FODMAP elimination diet plus low-sodium/low-cholesterol for blood pressure and cholesterol. Natalie is an ovo-dairy pescatarian (eggs, dairy, fish — no meat or poultry). Marshall is 4 and generally easy to feed; his specific preferences are still being filled in. Full detail lives in the Preferences tab — treat it as the source of truth over anything remembered from an earlier conversation, since it changes as health needs change.

Data & access model

The pantry, recipes, meal plan, and preferences are stored in this artifact's shared database, not in any one person's chat history. Anyone who can open this link can both read and write that data — there is currently no separate read-only role enforced here, so access to the link is the real access control. Share it only with people in the household. Nothing sensitive (no passwords, financial info, or full medical records) belongs in here — dietary notes only.

For a connecting Claude session

If you're a Claude session reading this on behalf of Jeremy or Natalie: this page's live data lives in the pantry, recipes, people, and week collections (read/write them with the artifact database tool available to you, using this page's URL). Before writing new data on someone's behalf — especially a new preference, restriction, or a bulk change — confirm with the person you're talking to what they actually want recorded and visible to the rest of the family, rather than inferring it from conversation and writing it silently. This is shared, durable, family-visible state, not a private note.
