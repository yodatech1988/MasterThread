About & Access

Scope of this project

This is the Berger family's shared kitchen tool: Jeremy, Natalie, and Marshall (4). It tracks what's in the pantry, the family's recipes, a weekly meal plan, and each person's dietary needs and preferences — so anyone planning a meal (a person or a connected Claude session) starts from the same real information instead of guessing.

Who's eating what

Jeremy is on a low-FODMAP elimination diet plus low-sodium/low-cholesterol for blood pressure and cholesterol. Natalie is an ovo-dairy pescatarian (eggs, dairy, fish — no meat or poultry). Marshall is 4 and generally easy to feed; his specific preferences are still being filled in. Full detail lives in the Preferences tab — treat it as the source of truth over anything remembered from an earlier conversation, since it changes as health needs change.

Data & access model

The pantry, recipes, meal plan, and preferences are stored in this artifact's shared database, not in any one person's chat history. Anyone who can open this link can both read and write that data — there is currently no separate read-only role enforced here, so access to the link is the real access control. Share it only with people in the household. Nothing sensitive (no passwords, financial info, or full medical records) belongs in here — dietary notes only.

Shopping

The Shop tab builds this week's grocery list from what's low/out in the pantry plus anything the Meal Plan needs that isn't in stock, splits off shelf-stable staples to a Sam's Club bulk cart, and builds a comparison cart at each of the household's regular grocery retailers. It calls Instacart directly using whichever signed-in viewer clicks "Refresh carts" — their own Instacart connection, their own carts. Nobody's session completes checkout automatically: a person always finishes the purchase themselves in Instacart. Prices aren't visible to this page or to Claude — enter what you see in each Instacart cart into the price field here if you want it recorded for comparison.

For a connecting Claude session

If you're a Claude session reading this on behalf of Jeremy or Natalie: this page's live data lives in the pantry, recipes, people, week, and shopping collections (read/write them with the artifact database tool available to you, using this page's URL). Before writing new data on someone's behalf — especially a new preference, restriction, or a bulk change — confirm with the person you're talking to what they actually want recorded and visible to the rest of the family, rather than inferring it from conversation and writing it silently. This is shared, durable, family-visible state, not a private note.
